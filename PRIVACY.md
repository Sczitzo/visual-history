# Privacy policy

Visual History reads the history already in your Brave profile to show your visits. It leaves Brave’s history untouched and keeps its previews, stars, and settings in that profile. Visual History has no accounts, analytics, or data uploads.

## Automatic previews

Screenshot previews are off until you enable them. Brave asks for access to all websites so the extension can take a screenshot of the web page you’re viewing after it loads. The extension skips private windows, local files, and common sensitive pages like sign-in and payment pages. It can miss private information on an ordinary page. Add any site you want it to skip under **Settings → Excluded sites**.

Saved images use about 1 MB of space at most. Images expire after seven days and are removed the next time Visual History checks its saved images. Brave may use additional space to store the images and their details. When Brave reports a history deletion, Visual History clears saved images.

## Your controls

- **Pause previews** stops new screenshots. You can also withdraw the website permission in Brave.
- **Clear thumbnails** deletes all screenshot previews. History and stars stay available.
- **Excluded sites** removes saved screenshots for those sites and prevents new ones.
